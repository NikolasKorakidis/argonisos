ARGONISOS

PROLOGUE · THE ISLAND OF THE WRECK

The Story So Far

A storm split your ship on the rocks. You wake on a small island in the Aegean with nothing but a torn tunic. An old hermit, Nestor, has lived here for years. He knows the island, the beasts that hunt at night, and the cursed ruins on the hill.

Survive a few days. Gather. Hunt. Fight what guards the ruins. Build a raft and sail south to Greece, where your real journey begins.

Prologue Loop

Gather → Craft tools → Make fire → Hunt & cook → Survive the night → Clear the ruins → Build the raft → Sail.

Length

~20–30 minutes. One in-game day = 5 real minutes. Wolves come at night.

Survival

Health regenerates while your hunger is above half.
Hunger goes down over time. At zero you lose health.
Stamina runs sprinting, jumping and swings.
Eating raw meat hurts you, so cook it at a fire.

Tools

Hands: slow chopping and mining, weak punches.
Stone Axe: fells trees and breaks rocks fast.
Spear: long reach and heavy damage. Use it for hunting and fights.

Tips

Pick up branches and pebbles from the ground to get started.
Bushes give berries and fiber. Reeds near the shore give fiber.
Boars charge back when you hit them. Rabbits run.
Follow the gold marker ◆ to find your quest target.

Vision

Argonisos is a third-person survival-building RPG set in a stylized Ancient Greece, playable solo or in co-op. Its references are Valheim (the loop and the loneliness), Raft and The Forest (bootstrapping from nothing) and Assassin's Creed Odyssey (the Aegean fantasy). This prologue is the tutorial slice. It teaches every core verb in a small, safe-feeling space, then gives the player the open sea as a reward.

Pillars

1. Earned progress: every tool comes from something you gathered.
2. Day is safe, night is not: the day/night cycle sets the rhythm.
3. Myth at the edges: the ruins and skeletons hint at a larger world of gods and monsters.
4. The journey: the sea and the raft point you onward.

Core Loop (this prototype)

Minute to minute: move, swing, collect, dodge.
Hour to hour: craft the next tool, cook, prepare for night.
Session: quest chain → raft → departure.
Test here: resource pacing, swing feel, night pressure, quest clarity.

Art Direction

Low-poly, flat-shaded world with soft golden-hour light. Characters use smooth, rounded, Pixar-style proportions (big heads, big eyes) so they read clearly against the faceted environment. Terracotta roofs, white plaster, Aegean blue and olive greens.
