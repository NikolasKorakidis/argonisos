# Argonisos: sound and music review (2026-11-20)

> Kept from the review round of 20 November 2026. The screenshots, probe scripts and raw logs it names were in the
> reviewer's scratch folder and are not kept; the line numbers are as of the commit it names.

This was a review only: no files under `src/`, `tests/`, `tools/` or `docs/` were changed.

What I read: CLAUDE.md, `docs/plans/audio.md`, `src/audio/sound.js`, `src/audio/music.js`, `tools/assets/sounds.mjs`, the manifest's
`sounds`, `soundGroups` and `music`, `assets/sounds/pack/sounds.json` and `tests/suites/audio.mjs`. I also traced every `sound(...)`
call site (about 110 of them) back to `SFX`.

What I measured:
1. **ffmpeg EBU R128** on all 157 converted files: integrated loudness, the loudest momentary reading and true peak. The table is in
   `scratchpad/loud.txt`.
2. **A short headless probe** (`scratchpad/probe.mjs`, raw results in `scratchpad/probe.json`). It used the test harness's GPU
   Chrome and ran for about 4 minutes. It took `AUDIO.meters()` in 7 scenes, then played about 60 effects one at a time with the
   beds soloed off, and counted the WebAudio nodes created. The machine's load average was about 25 at the time (other reviewers
   were running), so the timings mean nothing. The loudness readings do not depend on load.

All meter values are K-weighted with a window of about 0.37 s, in LUFS. "top" means the loudest moment on the bus.

---

## Ranked findings

### 1. Boss fights are over 10 LU louder than the rest of the game, and the compressor's automatic makeup gain is invisible in the code (bug / mix, M)
- **Evidence:**
  - `FIGHT.music = 3.6` is +11.1 dB. `docs/plans/audio.md:107` still says "5.6 dB up" (the gain was 1.9 before Patch 22).
  - The ambience goes ×0.5, which is -6 dB; the doc says -3.7 dB.
  - The doc's own measurement, taken before Patch 22 raised the gain: output -16.1 LUFS before the fight and -11.4 during it. It
    is louder still now.
  - Probe, each effect alone: a big slam at 15 m tops at **-8.7** on the sfx bus with its peak at **-1.8 dBFS** before the
    limiter. A boss roar at 20 m tops at -10.1. For comparison, an axe chop tops at -23.8 and a blade hit at -22.8. That is a
    15 LU gap.
  - Chrome's `DynamicsCompressorNode` adds automatic makeup gain (Web Audio spec: makeup = (1/full-range gain)^0.6). With
    `comp` at -16/3:1/knee 12 and `lim` at -5/20:1, that comes to roughly +8 to 9 dB. Measured: the ambience bus peaks at -15.9
    dBFS, while the output peaks at -11.0 with the master at 0.8 (-1.9 dB). That is about +7 dB that the code never mentions.
  - Every bus goes through the same compressor, so a boss's booms pump the music and the ambience down with them.
- **Cause:**
  - `sound.js:25` (`FIGHT`)
  - `sound.js:54-59`: one shared compressor, then the limiter; no makeup compensation
  - `sound.js:255, 271, 317`: boss volumes 1.1 to 1.25 on recordings already mastered at -13 LUFS
- **Fix:**
  - Aim for a boss fight output of about -16 to -18 LUFS. Set `FIGHT.music` to about 2.0 (+6 dB) and trim the boss one-shots by
    about 4 dB; the `sub` layers can carry the weight instead.
  - Make the master a true limiter: threshold about -1.5 dBFS, ratio 20, knee 0. Then either divide its makeup out with a gain
    after it, or replace it with a small AudioWorklet peak limiter.
  - Move the "glue" compression onto the sfx bus only, so the music and the beds stop pumping.
  - Update audio.md, then re-measure with `node tests/run.mjs audio`. The test has no check on boss loudness; add "output under
    -14 LUFS in the Minotaur fight".

### 2. The guide's chime and the grave's chime go through the music bus (bug, S)
- **Evidence:** `SFX.chime` is played through `pluck()`, which connects to `musicBus`. The probe shows the chime silent on the
  sfx bus (-120) while it is heard on the output. With the music slider at 0, the guided steps' "step done" chime
  (`guide.js:57`) and the grave's chime (`save.js:89`) are silent. They also sit under the music slider's 0.55 factor.
- **Cause:** `sound.js:177` (`pluck` → `musicBus`), `sound.js:325`
- **Fix:** give `pluck` a `bus` option. `chime` uses `sfxBus`; the lyre phrases stay on `musicBus`.

### 3. The rolling logs' sounds bypass the whole mix (bug / structure, M)
- **Evidence:** `harvest.js` builds its own WebAudio graph and connects it to `THUNDER.out()`. That is the master gain after the
  compressor and the limiter. As a result the rumble and the thud:
  - skip the limiter, so they can clip on top of a boss fight;
  - copy the effects slider by multiplying `SETTINGS.sfx` themselves;
  - are not muffled by `daze`, the indoor `shut` or the fight mix, and get no reverb;
  - never reach `AUDIO.log` or the meters, so no test can see them.

  The file also repeats a spatialiser (`heard`), a brown-noise buffer and a thud synth that `sound.js` already has.
  `Rumble` is torn down and rebuilt each time `l.touch` flickers on a bounce.
- **Cause:**
  - `harvest.js:134-170`: `p.connect(THUNDER.out())` at :152 and :163
  - `sound.js:414` (`THUNDER.out` is exported for the tests)
  - `harvest.js:516`
- **Fix:** move the thud and the rumble into `sound.js`:
  - `SFX.logThud({ at, k })`;
  - a general handle for "a looping, placed, live-controlled voice", e.g. `voice('logRumble', { at })` returning
    `{ set(at, k, f), stop() }`, on `sfxBus`, logged to `AUDIO.log`;
  - keep the rumble alive for about 0.3 s after `touch` drops.

  It is also the right home for the boss's charge rumble and the hand torch (#14).

### 4. Short beds loop audibly, and a bed never changes variant while it plays (repetition, M)
- **Evidence (`sounds.json` lengths):**

  | Bed | Loop length |
  | --- | --- |
  | `bedLeaves` (the whole forest's daytime bed) | 8.9 s |
  | `bedFire_1` | 7.5 s |
  | `bedWindMid` | 11.2 s |
  | `bedWindSoft` | 11.2 s and 14.3 s |
  | `bedWindStrong` | 15.2 s and 16.5 s |
  | `bedFrogs` | 19.6 s and 19.6 s (both exactly 19.573 s: check they aren't one take) |
  | `bedGrass` | 25.4 s |

  `bed()` picks one variant when the bed starts and loops it until the bed has been silent for 25 s. A day in the forest is the
  same 8.9 s of leaves, about 130 times, under the same 45 s of birdsong.

  The fires make it worse. `fires()` chooses a fire's variant by `f.id.length % n`. Every site fire is `'site0'` to `'site9'`
  (5 characters), so all of them (the temple's, the caves') get variant 1, the **7.5 s** loop. Your own fires with ids of the
  same length all share one variant too.
- **Cause:** `sound.js:380` (variant chosen once), `sound.js:396` (`f.id.length % n`)
- **Fix:**
  - Hash the fire id properly, or alternate the variants.
  - For beds under 30 s, run two copies of the bed at different offsets and rates (0.96 and 1.04), each with a slow random
    gain drift (period 7 to 13 s) that keeps their sum steady. That breaks the cycle without new files.
  - Every few minutes, crossfade to the bed's other variant.
  - Ask the team for beds of at least 60 s (see the recordings list).

### 5. The Minotaur's charge warning (the snort) and the summoned skeletons' bones can hardly be heard (mix / gameplay, S)
- **Evidence (probe, effect alone, momentary top in LUFS):**

  | Sound | Distance | Top |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | `snort` | 15 m | **-37** |
  | `pant` | 15 m | -27 |
  | boss roar | 20 m | -10 |
  | `giantStep` | 15 m | -22.5 |
  | `boneRattle` (bones clacking as they come) | 4 m | **-64**: inaudible |
  | `boneCrumble` | 4 m | -44 |
  | `thornThrow` (a dryad's thorn) | | -38 |
  | `thornHit` | | -39.5 |
  | an ordinary hit, for comparison | | about -22 |

  The snort is the cue that a charge is coming (`trial.js:391`). In the fight mix the music is +11 dB over it, so it is lost.
  The skeletons' rattle is meant to warn you of them coming (`creatures.js:217`).
- **Cause:**
  - `sound.js:352`: `snort` volumes 0.12 to 0.5 through narrow bandpass filters on brown noise
  - `sound.js:362`: `boneRattle` grains of 0.08 to 0.16 through filters with Q 5 to 10
  - `sound.js:280-281`: the thorn sounds
- **Fix:**
  - Raise `snort` by about 15 dB. Better, layer `growl` (the first pack's growl, slowed to about 0.55) with `slamHit`'s scrape
    for the hoof, until a bull recording exists.
  - Raise `boneRattle` by about 20 dB and the crumble by about 10 dB, and lower their Q to about 2 to 3.
  - Raise the thorn sounds by about 10 dB.
  - Duck the boss music by about 3 dB for 1 s on each telegraph (snort, zap, roar wind-up).
  - Add a test: in the fight, every telegraph's top is at least -24 at 15 m.

### 6. Blunt hits, the wrong-tool clink and the creature death sound are cheap synths, far too quiet, and the death sound has no place (mix / quality, S)
- **Evidence (probe tops):**

  | Sound | Top | Comparison |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | `hit` with a mace, club, fist or kick (synthesised) | **-36.3** | -22.6 for an axe: **14 LU** quieter |
  | `clink` (the wrong tool) | **-44.7** | -23.8 for a chop |
  | `arrowThud` (an arrow into the ground) | **-49** | |
  | `kill` (a 90 Hz triangle blip) | -34 | |

  The mace is a real weapon (`items.js`, `skill: 'mace'`), so clubs and maces feel weak.

  `kill` is played without `at` (`creatures.js:163`). A deer shot 40 m away "dies" in your ear. It is also the *only* sound when a
  boss dies (`trial.js:226`), the game's biggest moment.
- **Cause:** `sound.js:219-220`, `:213-214`, `:245`, `:251`; `combat.js:84`; `creatures.js:163`
- **Fix:**
  - Blunt: play `blockHit` (wood on wood) pitched down to about 0.8, over the brown thud, at about 0.6. Or play `slamHit` sped
    up for the mace, until blunt recordings exist.
  - Clink: play `stoneHit` (the first pack's) at about 1.4 for rock and `hitTree` at about 0.6 with a lowpass for wood.
  - Kill: pass `at: c.pos` and play a body-fall (`landGrass` slowed, or `boulder` very soft) instead of the blip.
  - Boss death: `roar({ boss, long })` slowed to about 0.7 and fading, then `collapse` and `blast`. Duck the music for it.

### 7. Most frequent actions have no sound at all (coverage, M)
Silent today:
- **Swinging a weapon or tool.** A miss is silent (`combat.js:76` returns false before any sound), and creatures' wind-ups are
  silent too. The first pack's `whoosh` is downloaded and decoded at start but never played.
- **The roll.** It plays `jump`, a 0.14 s puff of noise (`player.js:165`).
- **Swimming.** Footfalls are turned off in water (`player.js:55`), and nothing replaces them.
- **Eating, praying, sitting, sleeping and the emotes** (`acts.js:125-236`).
- **Wearing armour** (`game.js:109`: a toast only).
- **Opening the pack, the map or the build menu; choosing a hotbar slot; dropping or moving items.** Only the character creator
  calls `ui` (`creator.js:55-60`).
- **The guard breaking** (`combat.js:103`).
- **A piece damaged by a creature** (`build.js:471`: a burst of splinters only).
- **A torch in your hand.** It neither crackles nor hisses; placed torches do (`fires()`). Equipping a torch or a hammer plays
  the blade-draw scrape.
- **Hard landings.** `land` has no fall-speed scaling (`player.js:181`).
- **Sprinting and sneaking.** They don't change footstep volume.
- **Low health and stamina running out.** No heartbeat, no breathing.
- **Respawning.**

Fix, in order of value:
1. A swing whoosh on every attack: the `whoosh` recording pitched by the weapon's weight. Hero and creatures.
2. Footstep volume by gait: sneak ×0.5, sprint ×1.3, landing ×clamp(fall/8, 0.6, 1.6).
3. Swim strokes: `stepWater` slowed to about 0.7 on each stroke cycle.
4. Eat and drink one-shots.
5. UI ticks for the pack, map and build menu.
6. A cloth and metal rustle for armour.
7. The torch in hand on the fire voice (#3).
8. A crack for the guard breaking: `parryHit` pitched to about 0.7, plus a short `sub`.

### 8. Every hostile creature has the same cry, and creatures have no voices of their own (coverage / repetition, M; recordings needed)
- **Evidence:**
  - Hog, dryad, dryadling, skeleton and giant all play `enemyAlert`: 2 variants, each 5.9 s and 3.5 s long
    (`creatures.js:202`, `sound.js:248`).
  - Passive animals (deer, fox, rabbit) only get a 0.2 s bandpass "start".
  - No creature has idle calls, its own hurt or death cry, or footsteps; the giants are the exception for footsteps.
  - Every hit on a creature is a flesh sound, even an axe into a skeleton or a dryad (wood).
- **Cause:** `sound.js:219, 248`; `creatures.js:163, 202`
- **Fix, without new recordings:**
  - Choose the hit by the creature's material: `T.mat: 'bone' | 'wood' | 'flesh'`. Bone uses `boneCrumble` grains plus
    `blockHit`; wood uses `hitTree`.
  - Pitch `enemyAlert` per type (hog 0.8, dryad 1.15).
- **Fix, with recordings:** see the list below. The structure is a per-type sound table on `TYPES`
  (`voice: { alert, hurt, die, idle }`), picked up generically.

### 9. Music: tracks of about one minute, two biomes on duplicate tracks, five finished tracks unused, and loops that restart audibly (music, M)
- **Evidence:**
  - Track lengths: Pedias 48 s, Yleos 60 s, base 60 s, menu 60 s, story 61 s. The bosses' tracks are 175 to 183 s.
  - Yleos is byte-identical to the title screen's music (md5 ae30c9…). The swamp, the story and Acheron are byte-identical
    (27c1c6…). Every time you come into the forest you hear the title tune, and the marsh replays the opening.
  - `oros`, `sailing`, `nisia`, `sirenReef` and `erebos` are mastered but carry `play: false`.
  - Loops: `music.js:56` restarts a loop by starting a *new* `<audio>` element 3.5 s before the end and crossfading over the
    outro. On the title screen and in the character creator, the intro comes back every minute. The new element has to buffer
    before it sounds, so a dip in the crossfade is likely.
- **Fix:**
  - Use the unused tracks for places that exist now:
    - `oros` for the climb up Olympos (above about 150 m, or `PEAK.snow`);
    - `erebos` for the Giants' caves (`structures.inCave`) or the dead of night;
    - `nisia` and `sirenReef` for the shore at dawn and dusk (`g.amb.sea > 0.4`).
  - Give Yleos and Valtos tracks of their own (for the team).
  - Loops: cut a loop region in `sounds.mjs` the way it does for beds (loop points on a bar line, written to `sounds.json`).
    Decode the menu and boss tracks to AudioBuffers and loop them with `loopStart`/`loopEnd`, or at least set `el.loop = true`
    on a loop-ready edit.
  - Ask the team for land tracks of 2.5 to 4 min, or 2 or 3 per land; `mayPlay` already avoids repeats.

### 10. Indoors and in caves the world is only quieter, not muffled, and there is one reverb everywhere (spatial, M)
- **Evidence:**
  - `open = 1 - inside*0.75` lowers the beds' volume, but nothing filters the ambience bus. Far one-shots lose highs only past
    5 m.
  - Every wet send goes to one 2.8 s generated hall: the open meadow, the woods, a house, the Giants' caves and the labyrinth
    alike. Footsteps have `wet: 0` even in the caves.
- **Cause:** `sound.js:420-421` (`open`, `shut`), `sound.js:60` (one `makeVerb`), `sound.js:195` (steps dry)
- **Fix:**
  - A lowpass on `ambBus`: 20 kHz outdoors, eased to about 1.2 kHz with `inside`, about 2.5 kHz under a roof only.
  - Two reverbs: a short outdoor slap (0.6 s, low send) and a long stone hall (3.5 s). Crossfade their sends by
    `inCave` / `inside` / the labyrinth.
  - Give the steps a send of 0.15 in caves.
  - A cave bed (drips, a low room tone), for the team.

### 11. Ambience by time of day and weather is coarse (ambience, M)
- **Birds switch off at a threshold.** `night < 0.3 ? … : 0` (`sound.js:466`) is a step softened only by a 2 s time constant.
  The birds stop at one moment, and there is no dawn chorus.
- **Crickets come in at dawn as well as dusk**, because `LIGHT.dusk` is high at both.
- **The forest's birds are the meadow's birds.**
- **Fog, thick fog and dense fog change nothing.** `updateAmbience` is never told about fog (`game.js:178`).
- **The Olympos summit sounds like a meadow.** At 480 m in the snow you still hear grass, birds and crickets; there is no high
  wind.
- **After rain**, the trees don't drip.
- **The storm wall** gets the first pack's `loopStorm` (`sound.js:448`), and its recording has its *own thunder* baked in. Those
  claps have no lightning, which breaks the rule that thunder follows a strike. The file is also 11 LU under the other beds
  (I -35.4), so the wall's roar is mostly the synthesised brown noise.
- **Fix:**
  - Birds: `smoothstep` on `LIGHT.day`, with a dawn peak (×1.5 for the first in-game hour after sunrise).
  - Separate dawn from dusk with `LIGHT.elev`'s sign or the time.
  - Pass `fog` and `alt` (the height above the sea) into `updateAmbience`. Fog: birds ×0.4, a lowpass on far one-shots,
    `bedWindSoft` ×0.5. Above about 200 m: fade the grass, birds and crickets out and bring in `bedWindStrong`.
  - Use `wetness.js` to keep `bedDrops` under trees (×forest) for about 60 s after the rain stops.
  - Drop `loopStorm` and ask for a proper storm-wall bed.

### 12. The conversion's limiter crushes short one-shots, so variants and groups differ in level (asset pipeline, S/M)
- **Evidence (loudest momentary reading after conversion, against the group's target):**

  | Sound | Measured | Target | Note |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `mineHit` | -22 to -26 | -16 | in game, the pickaxe tops 5 LU under the axe |
  | `buildPiece_1` | -25.1 | -17 | `buildPiece_0` is -20.8 |
  | `fireStart_0` | -24.5 | -18 | `fireStart_1` is -19.7 |
  | `arrowFlesh_2`-`_4` | -21.5 to -22 | -16 | |
  | `bowStretch` | -21.2 | -16 | |

  Variants of one sound differ by up to 5 LU, so the random choice also randomises the loudness by ±2.5 dB.

  Their true peaks of -0.6 to -1.2 show the gain ran into `alimiter=limit=0.89`: transients flattened and the loudness short.
  Several files are left with true-peak overs: `stoneHit` +2.1, `creak` +1.7, `bedFire` +0.4/+0.8, `growl` +0.5,
  `arrowRelease` +0.2.

  The footsteps are matched by `volumedetect` mean (RMS including silence), not LUFS. Wood steps come out about 5 dB under the
  grass steps: stepWood tops at -28.5 to -33, stepGrass at -24 to -28.5. In the probe, wood tops at -40.7 against -37 for grass.
  Wooden floors should sound louder and more hollow, not quieter.

  The first pack's `foliage` is at -39/-41: unusable even as a fallback.
- **Cause:** `sounds.mjs:30` (`LIMIT`), `:89` (the gain to reach the target, then the limiter), `:77` (`meanDb` for the steps)
- **Fix:**
  - For one-shots, cap the gain so the true peak stays at or below -1 dBTP, and print a warning when a file falls more than
    2 LU short.
  - Then trim the shortfall per key with `vol` in `SFX`, or lower the targets for transient groups (gather and build to -19).
  - Match the steps by their loudest momentary reading (as the team's groups are), or by LUFS.
  - Add a `node tools/assets/sounds.mjs --report` that prints this table.

### 13. A long sound stays where it started (spatial, S/M)
- **Evidence:**
  - `play()` computes pan, volume and lowpass once (`sound.js:130`). `bossRoarLong` at a rate of 0.8 lasts 14.7 s;
    `enemyAlert` 5.9 s; `fallingTree` 6 s.
  - If you turn the camera, or the boss walks round you, the roar stays on its first side.
  - There is no occlusion (a roar behind a hill or wall sounds the same) and no front/back cue: a sound behind you is identical
    to one in front.
- **Fix:**
  - For sounds over 2 s with `at`, keep the voice (`out.s` already exists) in a small list and refresh its pan, gain and
    lowpass each frame from `spatial()`. Let `at` be a getter for a moving source (`() => c.pos`).
  - Behind the listener, take off about 25% of the highs: `lp *= 1 - 0.25*max(0, -dot(fwd, dir))`.

### 14. Patch 22 sounds: cooking can't be heard from across the camp, and nothing sizzles while food cooks (polish, S)
- **Evidence:**
  - `cooked` tops at -33 at 4.3 m. With `place(at, 3, 30)`, at 15 m it is about -10 dB lower again, around -43, under an

  - One `cooked` is about 38 `noise()` calls, roughly 190 nodes at once.
  - Meat on the stand makes no sound until it is done.
  - The skill bell (`skill`, -31) is a pure sine with one partial. It is fine as a placeholder, but beside the recorded
    `revealChime` it reads as a UI beep.
- **Fix:**
  - Use `far: 45` and about +6 dB for `cooked`. Cut the crackle to about 14 larger grains on a pre-rendered "crackle" buffer
    (made once in `init`) instead of 34 voices.
  - Add a quiet sizzle bed per heated stand through `fires()` (the same voice mechanism): highpassed noise, k 0.15.
  - Give the bell an attack of filtered noise and a third partial (×5.4, quiet) until the team's chime arrives.

### 16. Footsteps: missing surfaces, and a random grass/dirt mix (polish, S)
- **Evidence:**
  - `surface()` (`game.js:66-67`) has no snow (Olympos above `PEAK.snow` 372 m) and no mud (Valtos falls through to `'ground'`).
  - `'ground'` chooses grass or dirt at random on *each step* (`sound.js:195`, 60/40), so one surface keeps changing texture.
  - Bronze armour doesn't clink.
- **Fix:**
  - Choose dirt or grass by the ground colour or grass density under the feet.
  - Add `snow` and `mud` keys (recordings), and fall back to `stepSand` slowed to about 0.8 for snow and `stepWater` at about
    0.6 for mud.
  - Add an armour foley layer when `armor() > 0`.

### 17. Audio performance: little per-frame cost, but 11 synthesised beds run forever and every one-shot is decoded at start (perf, S)
- **Evidence:**
  - In steady state with the pack, the probe shows about **0 nodes created per second** in every scene: no per-frame synthesis.
  - There is no leak: finished one-shots are collected, `fires()` and `release()` disconnect their nodes, and beds free their
    buffers after 25 s silent.
  - However, the 11 synthesised beds are always playing: `windLow`, `windHigh`, `whistle`, `leaves`, `leavesLo`, `rainHiss`,
    `rainBody`, `roof`, `sea`, `fire` and `roar`, through 15 biquads, each with its gain updated every frame. Most are scaled
    to ×0.15 to ×0.35 under the recordings, or are at 0 (`sound.js:68-79`, `:429-461`).
  - The convolver runs all the time.
  - `loadBank` fetches and decodes **all** one-shots at start (`sound.js:103`), about 120 files, about 200 s of audio, tens of
    MB as float. That includes the first pack's fallbacks that the team's files replace (`door`, `creak`, `stoneHit`, `thunder`,
    `foliage`, `whoosh`), which never play.
  - The meter analysers (15 of them, fftSize 16384) stay attached forever once `meters()` is called (tests only).
  - `renderCapacity` and `playoutStats` aren't exposed in this Chrome, so I couldn't measure the context's CPU directly.
- **Fix:**
  - Build the synthesised beds only when their recording is missing, or stop and disconnect them after their gain has been 0
    for 10 s, the way `bed()` does.
  - Only call `setTargetAtTime` when the target changes by more than 1%.
  - Skip fallback keys whose replacement is in `META`.
  - Decode the long one-shots (`revealChime`, `bossRoarLong`, `thunder*`, `windGust`, `enemyAlert`) when first wanted, as the
    beds are.

### 18. Small bugs in `play()` and `duck()` (bug, S)
- **`STARTED[k]` is set before the audibility check** (`sound.js:128-130`). An inaudible far copy within `gap` swallows a near
  one.
- **`variant()` moves `LAST[k]` on even when the gap swallows the play.**
- **`duck()`'s `cancelScheduledValues(t)`** with a delayed `t` (far thunder) is overwritten by a nearer strike scheduled later,
  so the far strike arrives without its dip (`sound.js:187`).
- **A creature's vanishing thunder (`thunder(k, {at})`) goes on `ambBus`.** The ambience slider and the fight's -6 dB act on a
  combat event (`sound.js:287`).
